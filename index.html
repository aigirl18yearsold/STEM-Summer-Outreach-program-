ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Learning should not stop at the classroom door.

The STEM Summer Outreach Program is designed to give students opportunities to learn through exploration, collaboration, research, and real projects.

Participants are encouraged to become active learners and knowledge sharers — taking what they learn and bringing it back to their communities.
